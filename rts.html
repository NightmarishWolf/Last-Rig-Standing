Controls

Left-click / drag select · Shift adds · double-click: every one of that kind on screen

Right-click move · on an enemy: attack · workers on a free oil deposit: drill it (a new base, or a Wellhead inside a claim) · a yard selected: rally point

A + click attack-move · S stop · H hold · F the selection's ability (scans, missile strikes, recon flights)

Workers: B build menu, then O oil rig or a platform's letter (Shift-click to place several) · . next idle worker

Admiralty / Dry Dock: each ship's letter queues it · Bases: U upgrade (bigger claim, more platform slots)

Tab research (the tech tree) · N, M your commander's two powers

Ctrl+1–9 make a group, 1–9 select it (twice: jump to it) · Home your Admiralty

Space pause (orders still work) · Esc cancel / menu · wheel zoom · arrows, screen edges or middle-drag scroll · minimap: click to look, right-click to send ships

Oil: rigs pump it into storage; sell it on the market for money (each barrel sold knocks the price down; it recovers). Ships cost money and oil; ships and platforms cost upkeep.

Supply: every ship costs supply. Your cap is 10 from the Admiralty, +5 per Crew Quarters, up to 100.

Research: points come in every second (the Admiralty, Research Labs, and funding you pay for). Techs unlock ships, platforms and base levels, and make everything better. Crew Quarters, bigger claims and most warships need research first.

Winning: destroy every enemy base, or finish a Deep-Sea Fusion Plant.
